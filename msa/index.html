Standard Terms /
Master Services Agreement

Version 1.1  ·  Effective October 27, 2026

These Standard Consulting Terms ("Terms") govern services provided by TLHG Ventures LLC, doing business as Adalia Advisors ("Consultant"), to the client identified in the applicable engagement letter ("Client"). The parties agree to these Terms by signing an engagement letter that identifies this version and effective date (each, an "Engagement Letter"). Each Engagement Letter describes the scope, schedule, deliverables, and fees for that engagement.

1. Scope and Changes

Consultant will provide the strategic planning, facilitation, operational, and other advisory services described in the Engagement Letter ("Services") with reasonable professional care. Any material change in scope, schedule, or fees requires agreement in writing, including email. If an Engagement Letter conflicts with these Terms, it controls on scope, schedule, deliverables, and fees; these Terms control otherwise unless the Engagement Letter expressly identifies and modifies a particular provision.

2. Advisory Role; Client Decisions

Consultant provides business advisory services, not legal, tax, audit, accounting, investment, securities, or regulated brokerage services. Client will seek licensed professional advice as needed. Consultant does not guarantee a result. Client retains responsibility for business decisions and implementation. Consultant may reasonably rely on information Client supplies without auditing or independently verifying it.

3. Client Cooperation

Client will provide timely information, access to relevant personnel, necessary permissions, and an authorized point of contact. Delays or changed assumptions may require a written adjustment to scope, schedule, or fees.

4. Fees, Expenses, and Payment

Fees and any retainer, cancellation, or travel-time charges must be stated in the Engagement Letter to apply. Client will reimburse reasonable, documented out-of-pocket travel expenses approved in advance or reasonably contemplated by the agreed scope. Consultant will itemize reimbursable expenses and provide reasonable supporting records on request. Unless the Engagement Letter says otherwise, invoices are due within fifteen (15) days of the invoice date. Client must raise any good-faith invoice dispute promptly in writing and pay undisputed amounts when due. No late charge or attorneys' fee shifting applies unless separately agreed in writing and permitted by law.

5. Scheduling and Termination

Either party may end an engagement by written notice. Client will pay for Services performed through termination and reasonable, noncancelable expenses or commitments approved or reasonably incurred before notice. A prepaid monthly retainer covers the stated month and is not prorated on termination unless the Engagement Letter provides otherwise or law requires it. Neither party owes fees for unperformed future Services unless a specific, previously agreed cancellation or minimum-commitment provision in the Engagement Letter applies. Consultant will provide completed, paid-for deliverables and existing work in progress in its then-current form.

6. Confidentiality

Each party will protect nonpublic business information received from the other that is identified as confidential or reasonably understood to be confidential ("Confidential Information"). It will use that information only for the engagement and share it only with personnel, subcontractors, or professional advisers who need it and are subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations. This duty does not cover information independently developed, already lawfully known, lawfully received without restriction, or public through no breach. Required legal disclosure is permitted with notice where lawful. Confidentiality continues for three (3) years after termination, and for trade secrets as long as protected by law. Each party may retain copies reasonably needed for legal, tax, compliance, or professional records, subject to continuing confidentiality while retained.

7. Work Product and Materials

Client owns materials it supplies. Consultant retains ownership of its preexisting and independently developed methods, templates, frameworks, know-how, and tools. Once Client has paid all amounts due for an engagement, Client receives a perpetual, nonexclusive, royalty-free license to use, reproduce, and adapt the final deliverables for its own business purposes, including confidential sharing with its directors, employees, owners, affiliates, lenders, investors, prospective acquirers, and professional advisers. Client may not resell or publicly publish Consultant's proprietary methods or deliverables without written consent. Third-party licensed materials remain subject to applicable third-party restrictions, which Consultant will identify when material.

8. AI Tools and Meeting Records

Consultant may use reputable AI-assisted tools for research, drafting, analysis, and meeting notes while retaining responsibility for its Services and using reasonable safeguards for Client Confidential Information. Consultant will not knowingly permit AI providers to use Client Confidential Information to train general-purpose models without authorization. Consultant will not input passwords, payment-card data, protected health information, or sensitive personal identifiers into AI tools without Client's prior written approval. Consultant may record or transcribe a meeting only after notifying participants and obtaining consent required by applicable law; recording will stop if a participant requests it. Client may request that an engagement be conducted without AI tools or recording, subject to a mutually agreed scope and fee adjustment.

9. Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, neither party is liable to the other for indirect, special, consequential, exemplary, or punitive damages, or lost profits or enterprise value. Each party's total liability arising from an Engagement Letter will not exceed the fees paid and payable under that Engagement Letter, except for fraud, willful misconduct, Client's unpaid fees, or liability that cannot legally be limited. These limitations apply regardless of legal theory. This short-form agreement does not create a separate duty to defend or indemnify either party.

10. Independent Contractor; Other Clients

Consultant is an independent contractor and has no authority to bind Client. Consultant may serve other clients, including competitors, provided it protects Client Confidential Information. Consultant may use qualified subcontractors and remains responsible for their work and confidentiality obligations.

11. Publicity

Neither party may use the other's name, logo, testimonial, or engagement details in public marketing without prior written approval. Silence is not approval.

12. General

North Carolina law governs these Terms. The parties will first attempt in good faith to resolve disputes through direct discussion. Any lawsuit will be filed in a state court of competent jurisdiction in Guilford County, North Carolina, or the federal court with jurisdiction there, subject to applicable law. Notices may be sent by email to the contacts designated in the Engagement Letter and take effect on actual receipt. Neither party may assign an engagement without written consent, except to a successor assuming its obligations in a merger or sale of substantially all assets. These Terms and the Engagement Letter are the entire agreement for the engagement; purchase-order and portal boilerplate does not apply. If a provision is unenforceable, the rest remains effective.

13. Interim Nature; Future Replacement

These Terms remain in effect for an Engagement Letter that incorporates them even if Consultant later publishes more detailed standard terms. New or revised terms do not automatically replace these Terms. A replacement applies to an existing engagement only if both parties expressly agree to it in writing, identifying the new version and effective date. Otherwise, these Terms govern that engagement through completion or termination.
